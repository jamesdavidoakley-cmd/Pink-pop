# Rainbow Pop

A first game for Cas. One HTML file, no build, no assets: emoji for pictures, the browser's voice for speech, a tiny synth for sounds, and canvas fireworks and confetti on every win.

Five games, five stars each, then a big fireworks show:

- **Letters** - "Find the letter B". Three giant letter tiles.
- **Say it** - a big letter to say out loud. Tap it to hear "B is for ball". On browsers with speech recognition, tap the microphone and it listens for the letter.
- **Words** - a picture and three short words. "Which word says dog?"
- **Pictures** - "Find the house". Three big emoji.
- **Numbers** - counting to 5, and sums like 1 + 1 shown as apples.

Wrong taps just wobble. After two, the right tile glows. Nothing ever says no.

Open `index.html` in Chrome or Safari. On an iPad, Share → Add to Home Screen.
