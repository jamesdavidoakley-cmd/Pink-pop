# Tower Pals

The tower-building edition for 3 to 4 year olds. Stack blocks, snacks and little yellow helpers as high as you like, press **Done!**, then let the crew knock it all down.

One file, no downloads inside, no ads, no network. Sounds are synthesised in the browser.

## Play it

- **Phone or tablet:** open `index.html` from a web link, then *Add to Home Screen*. The service worker caches it for offline play.
- **Android, no link:** copy `index.html` to the device and open it in Chrome.
- **Computer:** double-click `index.html`.

## How it plays

- **Tap** a piece in the tray and it drops onto the top of the tower. **Drag** a piece to put it exactly where you want; a dashed outline shows where it will land. Nothing ever topples while you build, so it is never frustrating.
- Pieces: blocks with letters and numbers, planks, tall blocks, tubes, roofs, an arch, a dome, a bouncy ball, a star block, twelve snacks, and five helpers who stand in the tower and say "Oof!" when something lands on them.
- The number counts each piece out loud. The camera zooms out as the tower grows. Milestones at 5, 10, 15, 20 and 25 pieces bring claps, confetti, birds, a plane with a banner (a UFO and a rocket on the Moon).
- Drag a piece back to the tray to take it away; anything on top drops down gently. Tap a piece to hear its name.
- **Done!** brings the whole crew out for a Ta-da, then you choose how to wreck it: **Charge!** (they run in and crash into it), **Cannon!** (Tiny gets fired from a cannon after a 3-2-1 countdown), or **Sneeze!** (Mo blows it over). Pieces tumble, bounce and roll; blocks settle on their sides, helpers land dizzy with stars.
- Afterwards the crew eats any snacks that fell, takes a bow, and you can build on the rubble or press **Tidy up, build again!**
- Three places: Garden, Beach and the Moon, where gravity is low so everything floats and bounces.
- Gus the foreman waits by the fence; tap him for encouragement. Hold the basket to clear everything.
