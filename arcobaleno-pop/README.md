# Arcobaleno Pop

Rainbow Pop, in Italian only, for Casper. Same single file, same five games, same fireworks.

- **Lettere** - "Trova la lettera B". The Italian alphabet (21 letters, no J K W X Y), each with a word: A come ape, B come banana, C come cane…
- **Dilla** - a big letter to say out loud. Tap it to hear "B come banana". With speech recognition, the microphone listens for the Italian letter name (bi, ci, effe, acca…).
- **Parole** - a picture and three words. "Quale parola dice cane?"
- **Immagini** - "Trova la casa", "Trova il cane", with the right article.
- **Numeri** - "Quanti sono?" and "Quanto fa 1 più 1?"

The voice is Italian (it-IT). Open `index.html` in Safari or Chrome; on an iPad, Condividi → Aggiungi alla schermata Home.
