# Photos

Drop the real photos in here with these exact file names and the site picks them up
automatically (until then each slot shows the grey "PHOTO: …" placeholder card).

| File | Where | Shape | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| `home-01.jpg` | Home hero, rotated card on the brick stack | ~4:3 landscape, min 1200×900 | Kids mid-build at a table, arms in the air, bright primary-colour bricks in shot |
| `about-01.jpg` | About hero, left card | 3:4 portrait, min 900×1200 | Max holding his biggest build |
| `about-02.jpg` | About hero, right card | 3:4 portrait, min 900×1200 | Dad carrying brick tubs |

Kids' faces need parental consent; hands-only shots are the alternative.
Keep files under ~400 KB (export at 1600px on the long edge, JPEG quality ~80).
